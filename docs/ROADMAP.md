# Development roadmap

## Agreed product direction

Visitors begin with their own questions and examine Scripture in their own Bibles. The guide positively presents the biblical case from a perspective consistent with the Seventh-day Adventist 28 fundamental beliefs. No Ellen White quotations. Named alternative claims appear only when raised by the visitor, and are examined through contextual biblical study. The tool must leave the visitor in control of pacing, direction, and personal conclusions.

The journal records what the visitor writes, not an automated judgment that God has endorsed a conclusion. Earlier thoughts remain available as understanding changes.

## Before a production release

- Substantially expand the searchable collection beyond the initial seven areas. Map coverage to all 28 beliefs and to a defined, growing list of common questions.
- Review every exploration's whole-passage context, interpretation, strongest apparent countertexts, reading prompts, and tone. Keep editorial evidence and outstanding questions in the repository.
- Add claim-specific entry points where researched source material supports them. For now an unsupported named offshoot query points to the general method of examining a teaching; it is not presented as a finished refutation of that system.
- Test the exploratory experience with the owner: useful search matches, comfortable reading, optional structure, and meaningful journal review.
- Test on the owner's Galaxy Z Fold 5 and a physical iPhone, including keyboard, browser reload, export/import, installation, and Vault behavior. Automated Chromium viewport tests do not replace device tests.
- Review the encryption/storage implementation, recovery workflow, large-notebook performance, and accessibility with real use.
- Decide how the public version will explain its interpretive foundation. The About page is intentionally deferred during sole-user development.
- Deliberately enable hosting, configure the subdomain and HTTPS, remove development indexing restrictions, and update release labels.

## Later experiments

- Optional on-device AI using browser-supported inference. Keep authored guidance and ordinary search functional without it.
- Evaluate AI-assisted passage/topic matching and understanding estimates separately. Never treat doctrinal agreement as evidence of knowledge.
- Keep any inferred familiarity specific to a topic. Do not expose levels, pressure users through scores, or introduce compulsory lesson paths.
- Improve recovery/import ergonomics and optional passphrase changes without changing the notebook's local-only ownership.
- Adapt further Prayer Journal functionality where it serves actual study needs.

## Explicitly not included in this version

Remote AI, accounts, synchronization, automatic note interpretation, denominational About page, Ellen White quotations, mandatory lessons, leaderboards, knowledge scores, public release, or a claim of comprehensive doctrinal coverage.
